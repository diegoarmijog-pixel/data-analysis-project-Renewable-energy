    What does this mean?

    Shows how much electricity comes from weather-dependent sources (solar and wind) in the final year. Under 40% needs more clean energy, 40–60% is a balanced mix, and above 60% needs strong backup storage. Also shows whether the country generates more electricity than it consumes (exporter) or less (importer).
